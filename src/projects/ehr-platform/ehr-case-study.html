Care Plan Module

A reimagined clinical care plan experience — replacing a dense, non-interactive data table with a card-based system built around how clinicians actually think during a visit.

Cards over tables

Progressive disclosure collapses 10+ concerns into scannable groups — expand only what the visit needs.

Non-interrupting drawer

Goal detail slides in beside the list, not over it. Clinicians keep spatial context while reading more.

Safety-first anchoring

Patient identity is permanently visible. A 2-second glance prevents wrong-patient medication errors.

1
Before Flat data table — 6 columns always visible
Now Card groups — reveal only what you need

Clinicians visiting 20+ patients/day can't afford to parse dense grids. Cards let the eye land on one concern at a time.

2
Before No grouping — all goals in one flat list
Now Goals nested inside their Health Concern

A goal without its parent concern is clinically meaningless. Nesting restores the natural hierarchy of a care plan.

3
Before Status/Priority as plain text in columns
Now Labeled inline: Status · Priority · Progression

Column-scanning breaks flow. Inline labels keep all metadata on one reading line per goal — no eye travel across the row.

4
Before No priority-based visual differentiation
Now Left border colored by priority (High = red)

Preattentive attribute — clinicians spot high-priority items before reading a single word.

5
Before Clicking opens a full new page/modal
Now HC detail slides in beside the list

Spatial context is preserved. The clinician can still see all concerns while reading the detail panel.

6
Before Single action column — no inline modify
Now View Details + Modify on every goal row

Actions co-located with data = zero hunting. Reduces the number of clicks for the most frequent workflows.
